# CampusConnect advertiser guide

## Register

Open `/advertiser` and register with:

- Business name
- Contact name
- Email
- Password
- Optional phone, website, and description

New accounts begin with a **pending** status. An administrator must approve the
account before it can create campaigns.

## Log in

Use the registered email and password. Your advertiser session is stored in the
browser until logout or expiration.

## Choose a placement

Placements can include:

- Feed banners
- Institution communities
- Homepage top banners
- Desktop sidebars

Each placement has a daily price and date range. Campaign cost is calculated
from the placement's daily price multiplied by the inclusive number of campaign
days.

## Create a campaign

1. Open **New Campaign**.
2. Enter a campaign name.
3. Select start and end dates.
4. Select a placement.
5. Enter an advertisement title and optional body.
6. Enter an HTTPS destination link.
7. Upload an optional image.
8. Submit the campaign.

Campaign status starts as pending and requires administrator approval.

## Campaign statistics

The portal and reporting API can show campaign impressions, unique reach,
clicks, and placement data. Verify statistics after campaign approval and active
dates.

## Advertising standards

- Use accurate, non-deceptive business information.
- Do not link to phishing or malware.
- Do not collect passwords or financial information through the advertisement.
- Use properly licensed images.
- Do not target minors or sensitive populations with inappropriate content.
- Do not claim endorsement or student statistics unless verified.

## Payments

Payment status and confirmation are handled through the administrator workflow.
The public platform should not represent a campaign as paid or active until an
administrator has confirmed payment and approved it.
